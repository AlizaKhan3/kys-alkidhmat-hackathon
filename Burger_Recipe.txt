Burger Recipe

1. Beef Burger Patty

For the burger patty, we use beef mince.

The mince we use is 80% meat and 20% fat, which we get from the vendor.

For 1 kg of meat, we add:

* 1½ teaspoons black pepper — teaspoon, not tablespoon.
* 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste.
* For a juicier patty, we can add 1 to 1½ tablespoons butter. You can use Nurpur/ milkpack butter.
* If you want the patties to be spicy, you can add ½ teaspoon red chili powder.
* If you want a slightly different and spicier taste—not exactly the regular beef patty taste—you can also add Shan Keema Masala.

Patty Weight

The meat balls we receive from the vendor are already made and are around 80 grams.

However, because we add our own spices and ingredients, we weigh the patties again.

For a single patty, the weight should be around 70–80 grams.

2. Burger Sauce

For the burger sauce, we use:

* 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
* 1 tablespoon ketchup
* 1½ tablespoons barbecue sauce
* ½ tablespoon mustard sauce
* 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
* ½ teaspoon or slightly less paprika powder
* ½ teaspoon or slightly less black pepper
* A pinch of salt

Mix all the ingredients together. The burger sauce is ready.

3. Iceberg Lettuce

First, wash the iceberg lettuce once.

Then dry it properly.

After drying, chop it.

4. Caramelized Onions

Take one onion and cut it into round slices.

Add 1 to 1½ tablespoons of butter to a pan and caramelize the onions in it.

Cook them until they taste slightly sweet.

The onions should have a slightly sweet taste. If they taste bitter, they will not be enjoyable to eat.

Caramelized onions should always be cooked on low flame, not high heat, otherwise they will burn.

5. Pickles

For the pickles, cut the cucumber into round slices, just like you would cut cucumber for a salad.

For the pickling liquid, take:

* 1½ to 2 cups water
* 4 spoons vinegar
* Juice of 1 complete lemon
* 3 teaspoons sugar
* 1 teaspoon salt

First, stir the water, vinegar, lemon juice, sugar, and salt together.

Then add the cucumber.

Let the cucumbers boil for approximately 15–18 minutes.

As soon as they boil and you notice that the cucumber's color is becoming slightly darker, close the flame.

Keep the cucumbers on low flame for around 3–20 minutes.

6. Toasting the Burger Bun

We use burger buns for toasting.

Apply a little butter to the half buns and toast them in a pan.

Toast the buns slightly in the pan, and then use them for the burger.

7. Cooking the Patty and Melting the Cheese

For cooking the patty, add 1 to 2 teaspoon butter to the pan.

Place the meat ball directly on the butter in the pan.

Smash the meat ball once only to form the patty.

Cook the patty without repeatedly pressing or smashing it.

Do not flip the patty while the bottom is still sticky.

Wait until the bottom is properly cooked, and then flip the patty.

Each side of the patty takes approximately 8–10 minutes to cook.

Once the patty is cooked, place the cheese on top of the patty.

The cheese will melt over the patty within approximately 3–4 minutes.

Once the cheese has melted properly over the patty, the cheesy patty is ready for assembling the burger.

8. Assembling Burger

For assembling the burger:

1. Take the bottom half of the toasted burger bun.
2. Apply the burger sauce on the bottom bun.
3. Place the iceberg lettuce on top of the sauce.
4. Add the caramelized onions.
5. Place the cheese-covered patty on top.
6. Add the pickles from the top.
7. Add another layer of sauce slightly on top.
8. Place the top bun.

The burger is ready to serve.
