CREAMY CHICKEN ALFREDO PASTA RECIPE

========================================
1. BOILED PASTA
========================================

Ingredients:
- Water — enough to boil the pasta
- Salt — 1½ teaspoons
- Oil — 2 teaspoons
- Pasta — 1 packet
- A little oil — for coating the boiled pasta

Method:
1. Add water to a cooking pot and place it on medium flame.
2. Add 1½ teaspoons of salt and 2 table spoons of oil.
3. Bring the water to a proper boil.
4. Once the water starts boiling, add 1 packet of pasta.
5. Cook the pasta until it is boiled, but do not overcook it.
6. Strain the pasta using a strainer.
7. Immediately wash the boiled pasta with cold water.
8. Add a small amount of oil and mix gently so the pasta does not stick together.
9. Keep the pasta aside until the sauce and chicken are ready.


========================================
2. CREAMY ALFREDO SAUCE
========================================

Ingredients:
- Milk — 2 cups
- Cream — 1 packet
- Chicken powder — 1 tablespoon
- Salt — 1 teaspoon, or according to taste
- Black pepper — 1 teaspoon
- Chilli flakes — 1 teaspoon
- Organic leaves — 1 teaspoon
- Mixed herbs — 1 teaspoon
- Rosemary leaves — slightly less than 1 teaspoon
- Butter — 1 tablespoon
- Chopped garlic — 1 tablespoon
- Flour (maida) — as required to thicken the sauce
- Cheddar cheese — 2–4 slices

Method:
1. In a bowl, add 2 cups milk, 1 packet cream, 1 tablespoon chicken powder, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1 teaspoon chilli flakes, 1 teaspoon organic leaves, 1 teaspoon mixed herbs, and slightly less than 1 teaspoon rosemary leaves.
2. Mix everything well until the spices are completely combined.
3. Place a pan on the stove over low flame.
4. Add 1 tablespoon butter.
5. Add 1 tablespoon chopped garlic and sauté gently.
6. Do not let the garlic turn golden brown. Just sauté it lightly.
7. Add flour/maida and mix well.
8. Gradually add the prepared milk and cream mixture while continuously mixing to avoid lumps.
9. Cook the sauce on low flame for approximately 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it becomes creamy and thick.
10. Add 2–4 slices of cheddar cheese and mix until the cheese melts completely into the sauce.
11. Taste the sauce and adjust the spices according to your preference if required.
12. Keep the sauce aside for assembling with the pasta and chicken.


========================================
3. MARINATED CHICKEN
========================================

Ingredients for 1 kg Chicken:
- Chicken — 1 kg
- Garlic paste — 1 tablespoon
- Chicken tikka powder — 2 teaspoons
- Oregano — ½ teaspoon
- Mixed herbs — ½ teaspoon
- Rosemary — ½ teaspoon
- Chicken powder — 1 teaspoon
- Vinegar — 1 tablespoon
- Chilli sauce — 1 tablespoon
- Soy sauce — 1 tablespoon
- Chilli flakes — ½ teaspoon
- Black pepper — 1 teaspoon
- Salt — less than ½ teaspoon

Marination:
1. Add the 1 kg chicken to a bowl.
2. Add 1 tablespoon garlic paste, 2 teaspoons chicken tikka powder, ½ teaspoon oregano, ½ teaspoon mixed herbs, ½ teaspoon rosemary, 1 teaspoon chicken powder, 1 tablespoon vinegar, 1 tablespoon chilli sauce, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, ½ teaspoon chilli flakes, 1 teaspoon black pepper, and less than ½ teaspoon salt.
3. Mix everything thoroughly so the spices and sauces coat the chicken evenly.
4. Marinate the chicken for 15 minutes.

Cooking the Chicken:
1. Place a pan on the stove over low flame.
2. Add the marinated chicken to the pan.
3. Cook the chicken for approximately 15 minutes.
4. Take the remaining spices/marinade left in the bowl and add a little water to it.
5. Mix well and add this mixture to the chicken in the pan.
6. Continue cooking the chicken on low to medium flame until it is properly cooked and the spices are well incorporated.


========================================
4. FINAL ASSEMBLY — CREAMY CHICKEN PASTA
========================================

Method:
1. Once the Alfredo sauce and chicken are ready, add the boiled pasta to the prepared sauce.
2. Mix gently until all the pasta is evenly coated with the sauce.
3. Add the cooked chicken.
4. Mix everything together carefully.
5. Cook the pasta, sauce, and chicken together for approximately 10 minutes on low flame.
6. Stir gently during cooking to prevent the pasta from sticking to the bottom.
7. Do not overcook the pasta.

Important Note:
If the pasta has already become soft or slightly overcooked during boiling, reduce the final cooking time in the sauce. Do not cook it for the full 10 minutes if it will make the pasta too soft.

The final pasta should be creamy, well-coated, and properly combined with the chicken without becoming mushy.
