1. Brand Origin & Business Structure

 Smash & Sauce kab shuru hua aur isko start karne ka shoq aur inspiration kya thi?

Q10. What is your exact beef mince formula, and why is it prepared this way?
 Aapke beef mince ka exact formula kya hai aur isko aise tayyar kyun kiya jata hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Strictly 80% lean beef and 20% natural fat, passed through the mincer twice. The fat keeps the burger juicy and
caramelized; double mincing gives a fine, cohesive binder so the patty never breaks on the griddle, with no egg or
breadcrumbs needed. Keep mince chilled at 2-4°C.
JAWAB (RU)
 Fixed formula: 80% gosht aur 20% charbi, do dafa bareek mince. Charbi juicy aur crispy crust deti hai; do dafa mince
se gosht jur jata hai aur taway par tootta nahi, anda ya breadcrumbs ki zaroorat nahi. Mince ko 2-4°C par thanda
rakhein.
Q11. What are the patty weights for Classic and Double Smash Burgers?
 Classic aur Double Smash Burgers ki patties ka wazan kitna hota hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Each chilled raw ball weighs 85-90 g. Classic Smash: 1 patty (85-90 g). Double Smash: 2 patties (170-180 g raw
beef).
JAWAB (RU)
 Har thandi kachi ball 85-90 gram ki hoti hai. Classic: 1 patty (85-90 g). Double: 2 patties (170-180 g gosht).
Q12. How do you prepare your homemade pickles?
 Aapke burger ke pickles kaise bante hain?
ANSWER (EN)
Made in-house, never commercial. Thinly sliced fresh cucumbers pickled in a brine of white vinegar, filtered water,
sea salt, mustard seeds and subtle aromatics. They give crunch and sharp acidity that cuts the richness of the beef
and cheese.
JAWAB (RU)
 Pickles market ke nahi, hamari apni recipe se bante hain. Bareek kate kheeron ko sirke, paani, namak, rai aur
masalon ke brine mein pickle kiya jata hai. Yeh crunchy rehte hain aur beef aur cheese ke bhari-pan ko khattaas se
balance karte hain.
Q13. How do you prepare the caramelized onions?
 Aap caramelised onions kaise tayyar karte hain?
ANSWER (EN)
Thinly sliced onions cooked slowly in pure butter over low flame for 35-45 minutes with a pinch of salt. Never
rushed on high heat, never any sugar. Slow cooking lets the natural sugars break down and brown deeply.
JAWAB (RU)
 Bareek kati pyaaz ko khalis makhan mein halki aanch par 35-45 minute pakayein, sirf ek chutki namak. Tez aanch
nahi, cheeni nahi. Halki aanch se pyaaz ki qudrati meethas nikal kar deep golden brown hoti hai.
Q14. What is the step-by-step griddle searing technique?
 Taway par burger patty pakane ka step-by-step tareeqa kya hai?
ANSWER (EN)
  Heat a heavy flat cast-iron griddle to 220-240°C until light wisps of smoke appear.
  Place the cold 85-90 g ball on the dry, un-oiled griddle.
  Cover with a small square of parchment/butter paper.
  Press with the heavy smash tool using full body weight for 10 seconds until paper-thin with ragged, lacy edges.
  Remove the paper; season immediately with fine salt and cracked black pepper.
  Sear side 1 undisturbed for 60-90 seconds until edges are dark brown and crispy.
  Slide a stiff, sharp metal scraper fully under the crust and flip in one clean motion.
  Immediately lay a cheddar slice on the hot patty.
  Cook side 2 for only 30-45 seconds until the cheese melts.
  Remove immediately. Never press the patty after flipping.
 JAWAB (RU)
  Griddle ko 220-240°C par garam karein jab tak halka dhuwan na uthay.
  Thandi 85-90 g ball ko baghair oil ke garam taway par rakhein.

5. Signature Alfredo Penne Pasta
Q16. Which pasta brand and shape do you use, and why?
 Aap pasta ke liye kaunsa brand aur shape use karte hain aur kyun?
ANSWER (EN)
Exclusively Penne from Bake Parlour or Regia. The tubular, ridged shape (penne rigate) holds thick cream sauce
outside and traps cheese inside. Both brands survive high-heat tossing without breaking or turning mushy.
JAWAB (RU)
 Sirf Bake Parlour ya Regia ki Penne. Nali-numa lakeeron wali shape sauce ko andar aur bahar pakad kar rakhti hai.
Dono brands tossing mein toot-te nahi aur halwa nahi bante.
Q17. How do you boil the penne to a true al dente stage?
 Penne pasta ko boil karne aur al dente rakhne ka sahi tareeqa kya hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and salt it generously (it should taste seasoned). Boil the penne 8-9
minutes (check 1 minute before the packet time): tender outside, firm white core. Drain and reserve half a cup of
starchy pasta water. Never rinse with cold tap water.
JAWAB (RU)
 Bade pateeley mein paani khoob ubaalein aur achha namak daalein. Penne 8-9 minute pakayein (packet time se 1
minute pehle check). Bahar se naram, andar se halka sakht. Paani nithar kar aadha cup pasta water bacha lein.
Thande paani se hargiz na dhoein.

Q18. How do you cook and season the chicken for the Alfredo?
 Alfredo pasta ke liye chicken ko kaise season aur cook kiya jata hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Boneless breast diced into bite-sized cubes and marinated in salt, cracked black pepper, garlic powder and dried
Italian oregano/thyme. Saute in a hot pan with a little butter/oil over medium-high flame for 5-7 minutes until golden
at the edges and juicy inside. Set aside.
JAWAB (RU)
 Boneless chicken breast ke chhote cubes ko namak, kali mirch, garlic powder aur oregano ke saath marinate karein.
Garam pan mein thode makhan/oil ke saath medium-high aanch par 5-7 minute saute karein taake golden aur juicy
rahe. Alag rakh lein.
Q19. How do you build the Signature Alfredo white sauce, and what about the secret spices?
 Signature Alfredo white sauce kaise banti hai aur secret spices ka kya maamla hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Melt 1 tablespoon pure butter over medium heat. Saute finely minced garlic for 30 seconds. Pour in chilled dairy
cream and whole milk, stirring gently, and bring to a gentle simmer. Add our in-house secret spice blend
(proprietary, never disclosed), plus black pepper and salt. Fold in grated cheddar and mozzarella with 2-3
tablespoons of reserved pasta water. Whisk until smooth, velvety and clinging to the back of a spoon. Toss in the
penne and chicken for 60 seconds.
JAWAB (RU)
 Pan mein 1 chamach makhan pighlayen, bareek lehsan 30 second fry karein. Dairy cream aur doodh daal kar halki
aanch par pakayein. Hamara secret spice blend (khufia recipe), kali mirch aur namak shamil karein. Cheddar aur
mozzarella aur 2-3 chamach pasta water mila kar chalayein. Sauce gaarhi aur makhmali ho jaye to penne aur
chicken daal kar 1 minute mix karein.

7. Packaging, Branding & Delivery Delay Protocols
Q21. What materials do you use to wrap the burgers, and when do you use each?
 Burgers ko wrap karne ke liye kaunsa material use hota hai aur kab kaunsa lagana hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Two materials: butter paper (standard, everyday handling) and foil paper. Use foil when maximum heat retention is
needed, such as cold weather or distant drop-offs, because it keeps the cheese melted and the bun warm.
JAWAB (RU)
 Do materials: butter paper (roz ki wrapping) aur foil paper. Foil tab lagayein jab garmi zyada der rakhni ho, khaas tor
par door ki delivery ya thand ke mausam mein.
Q22. What containers are used for packing Alfredo Penne Pasta?
 Alfredo pasta ko pack karne ke liye kaunse boxes use hote hain?
ANSWER (EN)
Food-grade, leak-resistant plastic boxes: 500 ml for single orders (Rs. 699) and 1000 ml for large/sharing orders
(Rs. 1,299). Press the snap-lock lid on all four corners until it clicks, so no cream sauce can leak.
JAWAB (RU)
 Food-grade plastic boxes: 500 ml single ke liye (Rs. 699), 1000 ml sharing ke liye (Rs. 1,299). Dhakkan chaaron
konon se daba kar lock karein jab tak click na aaye taake sauce bahar na niklay.
Q23. What is your bag packing procedure and branding standard?
 Final packing aur branding ka standard SOP kya hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Place wrapped burgers and locked pasta boxes into heavy-duty brown paper bags. Seal the top fold with the
branded Smash & Sauce sticker. The flat bottom keeps pasta boxes level and upright while burgers rest alongside
without crushing.
JAWAB (RU)
 Wrapped burgers aur locked pasta boxes ko brown paper bag mein rakhein. Upar wale fold par Smash & Sauce ka
sticker lagayein. Flat painda pasta ko seedha rakhta hai aur burgers dabne se bachte hain.
Q24. How do you handle delivery delays and keep food from degrading?
 Agar delivery late ho jaye to quality kharab hone se bachane ke liye kya SOP hai?
ANSWER (EN)
  Heat containment: wrap burgers in foil instead of butter paper if the rider is delayed or the trip is long.
  Sauce buffer: add an extra tablespoon of hot starchy pasta water to the Alfredo right before boxing so it stays
 silky and doesn't congeal or separate.
 Upright insulation: place the sealed bag in an insulated thermal bag, with the base kept level.
  Customer transparency: if the rider is delayed beyond 35 minutes, message or call the customer on WhatsApp
 with an honest update and rider tracking.
JAWAB (RU)
  Garmi bachana: rider late ho ya trip lambi ho to burger ko foil mein wrap karein.
  Sauce ko jamne se bachana: boxing se pehle 1 chamach garam pasta water extra mila dein.
  Bag seedha rakhna: sealed bag ko rider ke thermal bag mein seedha rakhwayen.
  Customer se rabta: 35 minute se zyada late ho to WhatsApp/call par update aur rider status share karein.

Q25. The burger patty turned out dry. What went wrong, and how do you fix it?
 Burger ki patty khushk (dry) ho gayi. Wajah kya hai aur iska hal kya hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Causes: (1) mince under 20% fat; (2) griddle too cold (below 200°C) so juices stew out instead of searing; (3) patty
pressed after flipping. Fix: verify 80/20, heat the griddle to 220°C+ before the meat goes on, and smash once for 10
seconds, never again.
JAWAB (RU)
 Wajuhaat: (1) 20% se kam charbi; (2) tawa thanda (200°C se kam) jis se juice beh gaya; (3) palatne ke baad dobara
dabaya. Hal: 80/20 mince, tawa 220°C+, sirf pehle 10 second smash.
Q26. The Alfredo sauce is thin and watery. How do you correct it?
 Alfredo sauce patli (watery) reh gayi hai aur pasta par chipak nahi rahi. Kaise theek karein?
ANSWER (EN)
Simmer on medium-low for an extra 90 seconds and add 2 tablespoons more grated cheese. If still thin, dissolve
1/2 teaspoon cornflour in 1 tablespoon cold milk, stir into the bubbling sauce and whisk 60 seconds until thick and
glossy.
JAWAB (RU)
 Medium-low aanch par 1-2 minute mazeed pakayein aur 2 chamach cheese daalein. Phir bhi patli ho to aadha
chamach cornflour thande doodh mein ghol kar daalein aur 1 minute chalayein.
Q27. The Alfredo sauce split or looks curdled and oily. How do you recover it?
 Alfredo sauce phat gayi hai ya oil chhor diya hai. Isko kaise bachayein?
ANSWER (EN)
The pan got too hot and the dairy fat separated. Remove from the flame immediately and whisk in 2 tablespoons of
warm starchy pasta water or cold milk vigorously; the sauce re-emulsifies within about 30 seconds.
JAWAB (RU)
 Aanch tez hone se fat alag ho gaya. Pan foran choolhe se utarein, 2 chamach garam pasta water ya thanda doodh
daal kar taizi se whisk karein; 30 second mein sauce smooth ho jayegi.
Q28. The caramelized onions smell bitter or look black. Can they be salvaged?
 Caramelised pyaaz kali ho gayi ya kadwi smell aa rahi hai. Kya yeh theek ho sakti hai?
ANSWER (EN)
Cannot be salvaged. Burnt butter and scorched onion sugars taint the whole batch. Discard it, wash the pan, melt
fresh butter over low flame and cook a new batch slowly. Never raise the heat to save time.
JAWAB (RU)
 Theek nahi ho sakti. Jala makhan aur jali pyaaz ka kadwapan poora batch kharab kar deta hai. Phenk dein, pan dho
kar taza makhan aur halki aanch par nayi pyaaz banayein.
Q29. The bottom bun becomes soggy before delivery. How do you prevent this?
 Delivery ke dauran neechay wali burger bun geeli aur naram (soggy) ho jati hai. Iska kya hal hai?
ANSWER (EN)
  Toast the cut side in butter until a solid golden protective crust forms.
  Lay the shredded iceberg directly over the sauce before the hot patty; it shields the bun from steam and drippings.
  With foil, don't wrap airtight while blazing hot: let steam escape for 20 seconds first, or wrap in butter paper and
 then foil.
JAWAB (RU)
  Bun ko makhan mein itna toast karein ke crisp crust ban jaye.
  Sauce ke baad pehle iceberg lettuce, phir garam cheesy patty rakhein.
  Foil ko foran 100% tight na karein; 20 second bhaap nikalne dein ya pehle butter paper phir foil lagayein.

Q30. What are the non-negotiable "Never" rules of the Smash & Sauce kitchen?
 Smash & Sauce kitchen ke woh kon se sakht rules hain jin par kabhi samjhota nahi hota?
ANSWER (EN)
  NEVER use lean mince: strictly 80% meat, 20% fat, minced twice.
  NEVER press or squeeze the patty after the first 10-second smash.
  NEVER add sugar to the caramelized onions: pure butter and low heat only.
  NEVER reveal the proprietary Alfredo secret spices or pickle brine recipe to anyone.
  NEVER rinse boiled pasta under cold tap water.
  NEVER reheat old pre-fried patties: every burger is cooked fresh to order.
 JAWAB (RU)
  80/20 aur double mince ke baghair koi mince istemal na karein.
  Pehle 10 second ke baad patty ko dobara na dabayein.
  Pyaaz mein cheeni na daalein; sirf makhan aur halki aanch.
  Alfredo ke secret spices aur pickle brine ki recipe kisi ko na batayein.
  Boil ke baad pasta par thanda paani na daalein.
  Purani tali hui patty reheat na karein; har order fresh banega.
 
Q31. How should the system respond to severe allergen inquiries (e.g., celiac, nuts, dairy)?
 Agar koi customer severe allergy ya gluten ke mutalliq pooche to AI ko kya kehna chahiye?
ANSWER (EN)
Escalate immediately and say: "This query involves medical health and severe dietary allergens. The Stand-In
system cannot authorize dietary safety. Please speak directly with the chef/owner before placing this order."
JAWAB (RU)
 Foran ESCALATE karein: "Yeh maamla medical aur shadeed allergy se mutalliq hai. AI system iska faisla nahi kar
sakta. Order se pehle barah-e-raast owner/chef se baat karein."
Q32. How should off-menu custom requests (e.g., beef steaks, zinger burgers, chicken
broast) be handled?
 Agar koi customer menu se hat kar doosri cheezein maangay to kya jawab diya jaye?
ANSWER (EN)
Respond politely: "Smash & Sauce specializes exclusively in handcrafted Smash Beef Burgers and Signature
Alfredo Penne Pasta. We do not prepare off-menu items, steaks, or fried chicken burgers."
JAWAB (RU)
 Adab se jawab dein: "Smash & Sauce sirf authentic handcrafted Smash Beef Burgers aur Signature Alfredo Penne
Pasta par focus karta hai. Steaks ya off-menu fried burgers dastiyab nahi hain."
