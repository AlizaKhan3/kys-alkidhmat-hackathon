So basically, maine apna khud ka small food business start kiya hai jiska naam Smash & Sauce Kitchen hai.

Main basically ek university student hoon, aur mujhe hamesha se cooking ka bohat zyada shauq raha hai. Actually, yehi main reason hai jis ki wajah se maine ye business start karne ka decide kiya.

Mujhe especially pasta banana bohat pasand hai. Mujhe pasta banate hue around four to five years ho gaye hain, aur Alhamdulillah, jin logon ne mera pasta try kiya hai unse mujhe bohat ache reviews mile hain.

Mujhe beef burgers banana bhi bohat pasand hai, aur mujhe ye banate hue almost one year hone wala hai.

Of course, food business chalane ke saath apne challenges bhi aate hain. Ye hamesha easy nahi hota, aur kabhi kabhi humein losses bhi face karne padte hain, lekin ye business ka part hai. Hum har cheez ko jitna manage kar sakte hain utna karne ki koshish karte hain, aur main apne expenses aur losses ka diary mein record bhi rakhti hoon.

Maine ye business apni aunt ke saath start kiya hai, aur hum 50-50 partners hain. Woh mainly business mein investment karti hain, jabke main actual kaam aur operations handle karti hoon, especially cooking aur orders.

Jab humne first start kiya tha, toh humein week mein around two to three orders milte the. Kyun ke business naya tha, is liye humne kuch local people se reviews bhi lene ki koshish ki.

Uske baad humein kuch aur orders milne lage, aur hamare first one and a half months ke andar humne around 20 orders complete kiye.

Ek cheez jo mujhe recently realize hui hai woh ye hai ke hamara purchasing process kitna different ho gaya hai.

Jab humne first start kiya tha, toh hum almost har cheez supermarkets se buy karte the, jaise Imtiaz, Naheed aur Chase.

Lekin ab kyun ke business thora organized ho raha hai, hum apne ingredients specific brands aur vendors se buy karte hain.

Hamare burgers ke liye hum usually Dawn aur Bake Parlour ke products use karte hain.

Pasta ke liye hum Bake Parlour aur Reggia use karte hain.

Chicken aur meat ke liye hamara apna vendor hai jahan se hum regularly purchase karte hain.

Fries ke liye starting mein hum normal potatoes use karte the, lekin ab hum 9mm potatoes use karte hain.

Burger packaging ke liye hum foil paper use karte hain, aur pasta ke liye hamare paas 500 ml aur 1000 ml boxes available hain.

Hamare beef burgers around Rs. 750 se start hote hain, jabke hamara 500 ml pasta Rs. 699 ka hai.

Hum homemade masala fries bhi offer karte hain.

Mere liye ek aur major challenge university ke saath business ko manage karna hai.

Main ek university student hoon, aur usually meri classes around 9 se 2 tak ya kabhi kabhi 9 se 3 tak hoti hain.

Toh sabse badi problem tab hoti hai jab humein lunchtime ke around orders receive hote hain, kyun ke us time main usually ghar par nahi hoti.

Agar order pre-booked ho, toh main usually raat ko hi cheezein prepare kar leti hoon taake sab kuch ready ho.

Lekin agar humein usi exact time par order receive ho, toh meri sister ya family mein se koi hamare WhatsApp ya business number ke through order receive kar leta hai.

Problem ye hai ke usually food actually main hi prepare karti hoon.

For example, agar pasta ka order ho ya humein apni pasta sauces prepare karni hon, ya even burger ke liye humein burger sauce prepare karna ho, toh meri aunt ke liye sab kuch exactly usi tarah banana difficult ho sakta hai jis tarah main banati hoon.

Is liye most of the time mujhe unko call karke recipe step by step explain karni padti hai.

Lekin phir bhi, kabhi kabhi confusion ho sakti hai, aur food business mein consistency bohat important hoti hai.

Ingredients ki quantity, sauce, ya cooking time mein even ek small difference bhi taste ko change kar sakta hai.

Aur honestly, university student hote hue home-based food business run karte waqt ye mere liye biggest challenges mein se ek hai.

Lekin saath hi, main is experience ke through bohat kuch seekh bhi rahi hoon — costing aur inventory se lekar customer handling, consistency, aur apni studies ke saath business manage karne tak.

Aur honestly, mujhe ye bohat pasand hai kyun ke ye business us cheez se start hua jo mujhe genuinely pasand hai — cooking.

Our First Investment

Jab humne apni first investment ki thi, toh actually humein kisi tarah ka loss face nahi hua.

Around one month and fifteen days ke baad, hum apni invested amount ka around 40% recover karne mein able ho gaye the.

Hamare paas resale aur doosre related expenses ke liye ek separate amount bhi side par rakha hua tha, is liye overall hamari first investment hamare liye achi tarah work out hui.

Toh hamari first investment actually ek achi learning experience thi, aur humne isko loss consider nahi kiya.

Our Habitt Stall Experience

Major loss jo humne experience kiya woh hamare Habitt stall ke during hua.

Stall ke liye humne proper separate investment ki thi. Humne sab kuch separately plan kiya tha, including stock, preparation, aur stall ke liye required doosre expenses.

Lekin unfortunately, jo sales humne ki woh hamari overall investment aur expenses ko cover karne ke liye enough nahi thi.

Kyun ke humne specially stall ke liye cheezein prepare aur purchase ki thi, is liye hamari investment ka ek significant amount loss mein chala gaya.

Organizers se jo information aur guidance humein mili thi, us se related bhi kuch issues the. Kuch cheezon ke regarding jo guidance aur understanding humein di gayi thi woh accurate nahi thi, aur uski wajah se stall ke during humein jo problems face hui unmein ye bhi ek factor tha.

Toh overall, Habitt stall woh experience tha jahan humein significant financial loss face hua.

Lekin ye mera first experience bhi tha ek proper food stall karne ka, is liye loss ke bawajood maine is experience se bohat kuch seekha.

Isne mujhe event planning, sales estimate karna, stock manage karna, organizers ki requirements ko samajhna, investment ko pehle se calculate karna, aur sabse important, realistic expected sales estimate ke baghair over-prepare ya over-invest na karne ke baare mein sikhaya.

Our Orders & Customers

Ek aur cheez jo maine starting mein notice ki woh ye thi ke hamare most orders un logon se aa rahe the jinhein hum already jaante the — mainly hamari immediate family, friends, aur unse connected log.

Bilkul naye ya unknown customers se orders milna starting mein comparatively bohat kam tha.

Toh ab hum jin cheezon par kaam karne ki koshish kar rahe hain unmein se ek ye hai ke hum apne immediate circle se bahar aur zyada logon tak reach karein aur aisi customer base build karein jo hamare paas is liye aaye ke unhein genuinely hamara food pasand hai aur woh dobara humse order karna chahte hain.

Ye bhi ek reason hai jis ki wajah se customer reviews aur feedback hamare liye important hain.

Kyun ke ek small home-based food business ke liye un logon ke saath trust build karna jo personally aapko nahi jaante, time leta hai.

Aur yehi woh cheez hai jis par hum slowly Smash & Sauce Kitchen ke saath kaam kar rahe hain.

Our Beef Patty

Ab hamari actual food preparation ki taraf aate hain, especially hamare beef burger ki.

Apni patty ke liye hum apna khud ka meat mixture use karte hain aur usko apni required quantity ke according prepare karte hain. Hum usually enough mixture banate hain taake hamara overall stock properly manage ho sake.

Patty mixture ke liye hum butter se start karte hain. Phir hum garlic aur ginger paste add karte hain, saath mein apni doosri basic seasonings bhi.

Hum apni patty ka base relatively simple rakhne ki koshish karte hain kyun ke hum original beef flavour ko maintain karna chahte hain.

Lekin kyun ke kuch customers slightly spicy patty prefer karte hain, is liye hum meat ki quantity ke according thodi si red chilli add karte hain. For example, jab hum one kilogram meat prepare kar rahe hote hain, toh hum controlled amount mein red chilli add karte hain.

Hum mixture mein required quantity mein butter bhi add karte hain.

Main one kilogram meat ki exact recipe aur measurements separately explain karungi, kyun ke is tarah ye samajhna easier hoga ke hum har ingredient ki exactly kitni quantity use karte hain.

Our Burger Sauce

Apne burger sauce ke liye hum mayonnaise,dippit bbq sauce, mustard sauce Worcestershire  aur tomato ketchup use karte hain.

Jo brands hum use karte hain woh bhi bohat difference create kar sakte hain. Agar aap low-quality brands use karein, toh taste significantly change ho sakta hai.

Starting mein maine actually kuch wholesale products trial basis par try kiye the kyun ke main cost reduce karna chahti thi.

Lekin honestly, woh taste woh nahi tha jo main apne customers ko sell karna chahti thi.

Is liye eventually humne different sauces aur ingredients use karna start kiya taake woh flavour create kar sakein jo hum actually chahte the.

Barbecue sauce ke ilawa hum mustard sauce, rosemary sauce, chilli sauce, paprika powder, rosemary aur black pepper bhi use karte hain.

Ye ingredients mil kar hamara burger sauce banate hain.

Main exact recipe, quantities aur complete process separately detail mein discuss karungi.

Our Pasta

Pasta ke liye hum usually pasta ko around 8–10 minutes boil karte hain, depending on the type aur brand jo hum use kar rahe hote hain.

Jaise maine pehle mention kiya, hum Bake Parlour aur Reggia pasta use karte hain.

Apni pasta sauce ke liye hum cheddar cheese, specifically Adam's cheese, cream, milk aur butter use karte hain.

Hum apni pasta sauce ke liye all-purpose milk bhi use karte hain.

Yahan ingredients ki quality bohat important hai kyun ke brand change karne ya lower-quality product use karne se pasta ka overall taste aur consistency affect ho sakti hai.

A Problem We Faced With Our Patty

Kabhi kabhi hamari patty fry karte waqt break ho jati hai.

Ek reason ye ho sakta hai ke hum accidentally patty mixture mein zyada butter add kar dein.

Ek aur situation ye hoti hai jab humein meat ko jaldi prepare karna hota hai. Kabhi kabhi hum raw papaya paste ki small amount use karte hain taake meat ko tenderize karne mein help mile.

Lekin agar raw papaya paste zyada add ho jaye, toh mixture water release kar sakta hai. Is excess moisture ki wajah se patty weak ho sakti hai aur frying ke waqt break hona start ho jati hai.

Actually, humein ye exact problem apne Habitt stall par face hui thi.

Cheese We Use

Apne burgers ke liye hum Adam's burger cheese use karte hain.

Personally, main Adam's prefer karti hoon kyun ke mujhe iski quality aur melting consistency achi lagti hai.

Usually, cheese ko properly melt hone mein around five minutes lagte hain, depending on how we're preparing the burger.

Apne pasta ke liye bhi hum Adam's cheddar cheese use karte hain.

Our Chicken Preparation

Chicken ke liye hum usually usko prepare karne se pehle properly marinate karte hain.

Marinade ke liye hum Shan Chicken Tikka Powder use karte hain, along with chicken powder, chilli sauce, soy sauce, black pepper, thoda sa salt, chilli flakes aur oregano.

Depending on what we're preparing, hum different seasoning mixes bhi use karte hain. Kabhi kabhi hum Rosemary products ya Falak products use karte hain.

Exact ingredients aur quantities us recipe ke according vary kar sakti hain jo hum bana rahe hote hain.

Our Homemade Pickles

Apne burgers ke liye hum apne homemade pickles bhi banate hain.

Hum cucumber le kar usko properly slice karte hain.

Phir around one cup hot water mein hum approximately 3–4 tablespoons vinegar, one small lemon, 2 tablespoons sugar aur half teaspoon salt add karte hain.

Hum sliced cucumbers ko is mixture mein daal kar around 30 minutes ke liye leave kar dete hain.

Uske baad hum pickles ko isi liquid mein cool hone dete hain.

Jab woh cool ho jate hain, toh hum unko refrigerator mein store kar dete hain.

Isi tarah hum apne homemade pickles prepare karte hain, aur unko refrigerated rakhne se humein unko properly preserve karne mein help milti hai.
